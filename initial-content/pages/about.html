RedressHub maps initiatives addressing colonial injustices and their legacies in Belgium. It is intended as a resource for communities, practitioners, researchers, educators, policymakers and others interested in understanding the dynamics, connections and gaps across the redress landscape. It aspires to be a living platform that supports and grows along with the people and organisations working in this space.
